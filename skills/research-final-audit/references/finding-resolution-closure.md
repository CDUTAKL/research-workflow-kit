# Review Finding Resolution

Use the issue table in `docs/thesis/final-audit.md` as the canonical record for reviewer, advisor, integrity-review, and audit findings. Keep the source report and manuscript version in the audit run log.

## Disposition

- `accept`: make the requested correction and preserve the evidence basis.
- `partial`: fix the supported part and explain the remaining limitation.
- `reject with reason`: retain the finding and give a concise evidence-based reason.
- `not_verifiable`: state what input or evidence is missing; do not silently mark it resolved.

## Closeout Rule

A major finding can move to `verified_closed` only after recording all of the following in the issue row:

1. The exact manuscript locator changed, or the reason no text change was appropriate.
2. The evidence or source artifact checked after the change.
3. A recheck result tied to the current manuscript version.
4. Any remaining scope limitation or unresolved part.

Recheck only the affected scientific/format lanes when a small change is isolated. Rerun thesis-integrity review when chapter structure or contribution logic changes, and rerun evidence checks whenever wording changes claim strength, numbers, citations, methods, or limitations. Do not accept or reject findings by reviewer count alone; verify each material point against the manuscript and source evidence.
